The Bot’s database/file storage, logging, retention, and deletion behaviour have not yet been verified. This page deliberately does not claim that information is or is not stored. 1. Information that may be processed

Depending on which commands and features are enabled and how the Bot is implemented, Kryvion may process information such as:

  • Discord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and command inputs needed to respond to commands.
  • Moderation-related details, such as the action requested, the target user, reason, and relevant message or event details, if moderation features process them.
  • Invite-related information needed for invite tracking, if that feature is enabled.
  • Music search terms, requested tracks, queue information, and playback-related data when music commands are used.
  • Technical diagnostics or hosting logs generated by the Bot or its hosting provider.

This list describes possible processing, not a confirmation that every item is collected or retained. The operator should remove items that do not apply and specify any additional data actually handled.

2. How information may be used

Information may be processed to execute commands, provide moderation and automod features, support invite tracking, operate music features, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and prevent abuse. The exact purposes depend on the enabled features and actual implementation.

3. Storage and retention

It has not yet been confirmed whether Kryvion persists information in a database, JSON files, other files, or only temporarily in memory.
